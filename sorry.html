I am sorry

Dear Helda,

I forgot your birthday, and then you had to be the one to remind me. That is not okay, and I am sorry. Your day mattered, and you should never have to remind someone to care about it.

There is no good excuse, so I will not make one. I simply let the date slip past me, and I wish I had been better.

What I want you to know is that I am genuinely glad you were born, and glad I got to know you. You are kind, bright, and strong, and the world is better with you in it.

I hope this year is gentle with you and full of things that make you happy. Thank you for being understanding, and happy birthday, even if a day late.

With respect and warm wishes,
Ravi
